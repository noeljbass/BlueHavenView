Frequently Asked Questions

Thirteen quick answers before you request your estimate.

What problems usually mean a window deserves a closer look?

Drafts, fog between panes, difficult operation, damaged frames, water concerns, or rooms that stay hotter or colder than the rest of the home deserve a closer look. Stephen can review the affected openings and explain what appears to need attention and what may reasonably wait.

Can I replace only the worst windows, or does the whole house need to be done together?

You may not need to replace the whole house at once. Stephen can review the windows that concern you most, compare a phased plan with a whole-home option, and explain which scope fits your priorities and budget.

What goes into the written price for my project?

Your written price depends on the number and dimensions of the openings, window style, glass package, colors, grids, hardware, installation conditions, and selected options. Blue Haven measures the openings, explains the proposed scope, and provides project-specific pricing before you decide.

What window styles, glass packages, colors, grids, and hardware are available?

You do not need to choose every detail before contacting Blue Haven. Start with the look, operation, and improvements that matter most. Stephen can help narrow the available styles, glass, colors, grids, and hardware for your selected window line. Available glass options may include insulated double-pane and triple-pane glass; Low-E energy-efficient glass; Low-E glass with argon gas; upgraded high-performance Low-E packages; tempered safety glass; obscure or privacy glass; tinted glass; and laminated glass. Grid options may include no grids, Colonial or traditional grids, Prairie, Craftsman or top-row, Diamond, and custom configurations. Depending on the selected window, grid types may include grids between the glass, flat grids, contoured or sculptured grids, and simulated divided lite. Available colors may include white, clay or tan, black, bronze, and additional interior or exterior finishes. Color-matched locks and operating hardware are available, with additional hardware styles and finishes depending on the product. Stephen will explain which combinations are available for your selected window and help match them to your home, priorities, and budget.

Could new windows make drafty, hot, or noisy rooms more comfortable?

The right replacement window and glass package may reduce unwanted air movement, heat transfer, and some outside noise. Results depend on the home, existing openings, installation conditions, and selected products. Blue Haven does not promise a specific utility-bill reduction or a completely soundproof room.

When will I know the installation dates and expected project length?

Timing depends on product availability, project scope, and conditions at the home. Raise any preferred timeframe early. After the order and installation details are confirmed, Stephen will review the expected installation dates and estimated project duration before work begins.

Are the product warranty and Blue Haven’s installation-workmanship warranty separate?

Yes. The manufacturer’s warranty covers qualifying products under the manufacturer’s written terms. Blue Haven’s separate Limited Lifetime Workmanship Warranty covers qualifying defects caused solely and directly by covered installation work, subject to its written eligibility, ownership, occupancy, transferability, labor, and exclusion terms. Stephen will provide the applicable warranty information before you order. Blue Haven’s Limited Lifetime Workmanship Warranty applies only to qualifying defects caused solely and directly by Blue Haven’s covered installation work. It applies to the original contracting homeowner while that homeowner continuously owns and occupies the original installation residence. It is not transferable unless Blue Haven expressly agrees otherwise in a separate signed writing. Windows, glass, frames, hardware, balances, screens, finishes, and other manufactured products or components are covered under the applicable written manufacturer warranty—not Blue Haven’s workmanship warranty. Manufacturer coverage, eligibility, limitations, labor allowances, exclusions, and transferability vary by product and purchase date. Stephen will provide the applicable written warranty information before you order.

Is financing currently available?

No. Financing is not available at this time. Stephen can explain the proposed project scope and current payment requirements before you decide whether the project fits your budget.

Who performs the installation?

Blue Haven remains your point of contact for the contracted project. Installation is performed by independent installation contractors, not Blue Haven employees. They work directly with Blue Haven and are expected to follow its installation requirements and quality standards. Blue Haven remains your contact for its applicable workmanship-warranty obligations.

Are cleanup and disposal included, and who handles permits or inspections?

Normal cleanup, removal of the old windows, and installation-related debris removal are included. The buyer is responsible for obtaining and paying for any required permits, inspections, or HOA approvals unless the written project agreement states otherwise.

How does the Buy 2 Windows, Get 2 Free promotion work?

For qualifying projects, the promotion is structured in groups of four windows: for every two qualifying windows purchased, the two free windows must be of equal or lesser value. Confirm eligible products and the current written promotion terms in your proposal. The promotion does not mean all labor or project expenses are free. All window styles offered through Blue Haven Windows may qualify, subject to the current written promotion terms included with the homeowner’s proposal.

Who qualifies for Window Cleaning for Life?

Window Cleaning for Life applies only to windows purchased as part of the qualifying Advanced Marvin Package. Covered customers receive one professional exterior cleaning each year for the covered windows. The benefit is subject to the written terms, including transferability, unused-cleaning expiration, exclusions, access, and scheduling requirements. The benefit is transferable. Unused annual cleanings expire and do not roll over. Screens, tracks, sills, interior glass, and unusually difficult access are excluded. Upper-story access is addressed on a per-project basis. The written proposal or program terms identify the covered windows and any additional scheduling or access requirements.

What happens during the free in-home consultation?

During the free in-home consultation, you’ll discuss what you’re noticing, review and measure the openings, compare suitable styles and product options, and discuss project scope, written pricing, warranty information, promotion eligibility, and expected timing. You can then decide whether the options make sense for your home.
