Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there fog between my window panes?

Fog between panes usually means the insulated glass seal has failed and moisture has entered the glass unit.

Is exterior window condensation bad?

Exterior condensation can be normal during certain weather conditions and may indicate the glass is insulating well.

Should I replace foggy windows?

If fogging is between panes and visibility or performance is affected, replacement is often the most practical long-term solution.
