Limited Lifetime Workmanship Warranty

Complete terms for Blue Haven Windows' limited workmanship warranty covering qualifying installation defects caused solely and directly by Blue Haven Windows' covered installation work.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Important Summary

This is a limited workmanship warranty, not a manufacturer product warranty.

Blue Haven Windows is the warrantor for qualifying installation workmanship only. Window products, glass, hardware, and other manufactured components remain subject to the applicable written manufacturer warranty for the customer's exact product and purchase date.

Our Commitment

Blue Haven Windows stands behind the quality of its installation work. When replacement windows are installed by Blue Haven Windows, we warrant that the covered installation work will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with the written installation contract, applicable manufacturer instructions, and generally accepted trade standards in effect at the time of installation.

This page summarizes the protection available to qualifying customers. Coverage is subject to all terms, limitations, exclusions, claim requirements, and manufacturer warranty provisions stated below and in the customer’s written contract.

The Four Parts of Our Warranty Commitment

1. Limited Lifetime Installation Workmanship Warranty

Subject to the complete terms below, Blue Haven Windows warrants covered installation workmanship against defects caused solely and directly by Blue Haven Windows’ installation work.

For purposes of this warranty, “Limited Lifetime” means the period during which the original contracting homeowner continuously owns and occupies the residence where the covered windows were originally installed.

This warranty:

  • Begins on the date Blue Haven Windows substantially completes the covered installation.
  • Applies only to the original contracting homeowner.
  • Applies only at the original installation address.
  • Is not transferable unless Blue Haven Windows expressly agrees otherwise in a separate signed writing.
  • Ends when the original customer sells, transfers, rents out, vacates, or no longer owns or occupies the property.
  • Covers only installation defects that Blue Haven Windows determines are attributable to its own covered workmanship.
  • Does not extend, restart, or renew following a repair or replacement.

2. Workmanship Guarantee

If Blue Haven Windows verifies that a covered condition was directly caused by defective installation workmanship, Blue Haven Windows will, at its option:

  • Correct or reperform the affected installation work;
  • Repair the affected covered installation component; or
  • Replace the affected installation material with a reasonably comparable material when repair is not commercially practical.

Blue Haven Windows is responsible only for the portion of the work proven to be defective and covered under this limited warranty.

3. Labor Guarantee

Approved labor reasonably necessary for Blue Haven Windows to correct a verified, covered installation-workmanship defect is included at no additional charge.

The Labor Guarantee does not include labor associated with:

  • Manufacturer product defects;
  • Manufacturer warranty claims, except when the applicable written manufacturer warranty expressly pays or reimburses such labor;
  • Routine maintenance or adjustment;
  • Conditions excluded below;
  • Work performed by another contractor;
  • Removal or reinstallation of blinds, shutters, window treatments, alarms, sensors, wiring, security devices, furniture, landscaping, siding, masonry, stucco, interior finishes, or other property not included in Blue Haven Windows’ original written scope;
  • Testing, engineering, environmental remediation, mold investigation, asbestos or lead work, structural repairs, or code upgrades not included in the original contract;
  • Service requested when no covered defect is found.

Any service-call, inspection, diagnostic, travel, access, lift, scaffold, finish-repair, or third-party cost not expressly included in this warranty remains the customer’s responsibility to the extent permitted by law.

4. Product Warranty Protection

Windows, glass, insulated glass units, screens, locks, balances, hardware, frames, finishes, sealants, and other manufactured products or components are covered only by the applicable written warranty issued by their manufacturer.

Blue Haven Windows does not manufacture these products and does not independently warrant that a manufacturer will approve or pay a claim. Manufacturer warranties may contain their own:

  • Coverage periods;
  • Registration requirements;
  • Transfer restrictions;
  • Labor exclusions;
  • Service fees;
  • Shipping charges;
  • Prorated coverage;
  • Glass-breakage exclusions;
  • Coastal or severe-weather limitations;
  • Color, finish, and normal-wear exclusions.

When reasonably possible, Blue Haven Windows will help the original customer identify and submit an eligible manufacturer warranty claim. Unless separately stated in a signed writing, Blue Haven Windows’ assistance does not make Blue Haven Windows responsible for the manufacturer’s obligations, decisions, delays, discontinued products, replacement-part availability, shipping charges, or denial of coverage.

If an exact product, glass pattern, color, finish, profile, or component is discontinued or unavailable, a reasonably comparable replacement may be used. An exact cosmetic match is not guaranteed.

Conditions of Coverage

Coverage applies only when all the following requirements are met:

  1. The claimant is the original customer named in the Blue Haven Windows contract.
  2. The claimant continues to own and occupy the original installation property as a primary residence.
  3. Blue Haven Windows performed the installation being claimed.
  4. The customer paid all amounts due under the contract.
  5. The customer used, cleaned, maintained, and protected the windows in accordance with manufacturer instructions and reasonable homeowner maintenance practices.
  6. The customer notified Blue Haven Windows promptly after discovering the alleged defect.
  7. The customer took reasonable steps to prevent additional damage.
  8. Blue Haven Windows was given a reasonable opportunity to inspect, diagnose, photograph, test, and correct the condition before another person altered or repaired it.
  9. The claimed condition is proven to have been directly caused by covered Blue Haven Windows installation workmanship.
  10. The customer has not made a material misrepresentation, concealed relevant information, submitted altered evidence, interfered with an inspection, or otherwise acted fraudulently in connection with the claim.

Failure to satisfy a condition affects coverage only to the extent that the failure caused, contributed to, concealed, aggravated, or prevented reasonable investigation or correction of the claimed condition.

  • Defective, deteriorated, undersized, altered, out-of-square, or inadequately supported wall framing, headers, sills, sheathing, masonry, siding, stucco, roofing, flashing, foundations, or other building components;

How to Submit a Warranty Claim

A customer seeking warranty service must:

  1. Notify Blue Haven Windows in writing within 30 days after discovering the condition;
  2. Provide the customer’s name, property address, contract or invoice number, installation date if known, and affected window location;
  3. Describe the condition and when it was first observed;
  4. Provide reasonably requested photographs, video, documents, maintenance information, weather-event information, and manufacturer correspondence;
  5. Take reasonable steps to prevent additional damage;
  6. Preserve affected parts when reasonably possible;
  7. Provide safe and reasonable access to the property; and
  8. Allow Blue Haven Windows a reasonable opportunity to inspect and, if covered, correct the condition before permanent repairs are made by another party.

Exclusive Limited Remedy

For a verified covered defect, Blue Haven Windows’ obligation is limited, at its option, to correcting the affected installation work, repairing the affected covered component, or providing a reasonably comparable replacement.

Blue Haven Windows controls the method, timing, personnel, and materials used for covered corrective work, subject to applicable law and reasonable scheduling. Replacement does not require an exact match when the original material or product is unavailable.

A customer may not recover the cost of third-party work unless Blue Haven Windows authorized that work in writing before it was performed, except where immediate emergency action was reasonably necessary to prevent imminent personal injury or substantial additional property damage.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the foregoing remedy is the customer’s exclusive remedy under this written limited warranty.

These limitations do not apply where prohibited by law and do not exclude liability that cannot legally be waived, including liability arising from Blue Haven Windows’ gross negligence, willful misconduct, or other nonwaivable legal obligations.

Some states do not allow limitations on implied warranties or the exclusion of incidental or consequential damages. Accordingly, some limitations may not apply, and customers may have additional rights under applicable state law.

Any modification must:

Statements in advertising, conversations, estimates, or sales materials do not create additional warranty obligations unless expressly incorporated into the signed customer contract.
