Two Different Warranties. Two Different Decision Makers.

A clear warranty explanation should separate product coverage from installation coverage. Blue Haven Windows helps homeowners understand both so expectations stay realistic before and after the job.

Manufacturer product warranties are issued and decided by Wincore or Simonton, depending on the product. Blue Haven Windows' Limited Lifetime Installation Workmanship Warranty addresses qualifying defects caused solely and directly by Blue Haven Windows' covered installation work, subject to complete terms, limitations, and exclusions.

The warranty applicable to your exact product and purchase date controls. Coverage can vary by product line, purchase date, registration, transfer rules, exclusions, and whether any labor is included by the manufacturer.

Compare The Two Protections

Manufacturer product warranties and Blue Haven's installation workmanship warranty work alongside each other, but they do not cover the same issues.

Manufacturer product warranties

Product and component coverage from Wincore or Simonton

  • Issued by the manufacturer for the specific product line, glass package, and purchase date.
  • Claims are reviewed and decided by Wincore or Simonton, not by Blue Haven Windows.
  • Terms can vary by registration, transfer rights, exclusions, service fees, shipping charges, and discontinuations.
  • Labor is not automatically included and may be limited or excluded entirely.
  • The applicable written manufacturer warranty for the customer's exact product and purchase date controls.
Blue Haven Limited Lifetime Installation Workmanship Warranty

Coverage for qualifying installation workmanship defects

  • Applies to qualifying defects caused solely and directly by Blue Haven Windows' covered installation work.
  • Limited Lifetime means the original contracting homeowner continuously owns and occupies the original installation residence.
  • Approved corrective labor is included when Blue Haven verifies a covered installation-workmanship defect.
  • Coverage is subject to full terms, conditions, exclusions, claim steps, and legal limitations.
  • Read the complete Blue Haven terms page for the full workmanship warranty.

Who handles what

Plain-Language Claim Routing

If the issue is with the manufactured window or component: the manufacturer warranty applies. That can include items such as insulated glass, balances, locks, frames, screens, hardware, or finishes, depending on the specific warranty.

If the issue was caused solely and directly by Blue Haven's covered installation work: Blue Haven Windows evaluates the workmanship claim under its Limited Lifetime Installation Workmanship Warranty.

If both may be involved: Blue Haven can help identify the likely path, but Blue Haven does not independently guarantee manufacturer approvals, part availability, shipping, or product-defect outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you request a quote.

Is either warranty unlimited?

No. Both the manufacturer product warranties and the Blue Haven workmanship warranty are limited warranties with terms, conditions, exclusions, and claim requirements.

Who decides a product-defect claim?

The manufacturer decides product and component claims under the written Wincore or Simonton warranty that applies to the exact product and purchase date.

What does Blue Haven's workmanship warranty cover?

It covers qualifying defects caused solely and directly by Blue Haven Windows' covered installation work, with approved corrective labor subject to the complete written terms.

Does manufacturer coverage automatically include labor?

Not automatically. Manufacturer terms can vary by product line and purchase date, and labor may be limited, reimbursed in part, or excluded entirely.

Where can I read the complete Blue Haven warranty terms?

Use the complete Limited Lifetime Workmanship Warranty page for the full terms, limitations, exclusions, and claim process.
