A no-grid update for a familiar home

The homeowner wanted brighter rooms and a no-grid appearance, choosing Wincore 7700 windows to replace the existing Simonton windows.

The initial order was for 31 windows. The homeowner liked the new windows and chose to add more, bringing the final completed scope to 36 window replacements.
