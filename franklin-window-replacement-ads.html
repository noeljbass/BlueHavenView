  • More even temperatures, fewer hot/cold spots.

    Multiple internal chambers act like built‑in insulation pockets, helping reduce heat loss and outside noise so nearby traffic, neighbors, or lawn equipment are less noticeable inside your home.

    (5‑chamber sash and frame design)

  • Extra defense against leaks and drafts

    The sill and locking design are made to move water away from the window and seal tightly when closed, which helps keep rain and outside air where they belong and reduces the chance of leaks or cold spots around the frame.

    (Simonton Sill drainage and Lap‑Lok weather protection)

Frequently Asked Questions

Thirteen quick answers before you request your estimate.

What problems usually mean a window deserves a closer look?

Drafts, fog between panes, windows that are difficult to open or lock, damaged frames, water concerns, or rooms that remain hotter or colder than the rest of the home are reasons to schedule an evaluation. Stephen can explain which windows appear to need attention and which may reasonably wait.

Can I replace only the worst windows, or does the whole house need to be done together?

Either approach may make sense. Stephen can review the condition of each opening, your comfort goals, the look you want, and your budget. You can then compare a phased replacement plan with a whole-home option without being pushed into work you do not want.

What goes into the written price for my project?

Pricing depends on the number and dimensions of the openings, window style, glass package, colors, grids, hardware, installation conditions, and selected options. Blue Haven measures the actual openings and prepares project-specific pricing before you decide.

What window styles, glass packages, colors, grids, and hardware are available?

Blue Haven offers multiple operating styles and product configurations based on the selected manufacturer and product line. Available glass options may include insulated double-pane and triple-pane glass; Low-E energy-efficient glass; Low-E glass with argon gas; upgraded high-performance Low-E packages; tempered safety glass; obscure or privacy glass; tinted glass; and laminated glass. Grid options may include no grids, Colonial or traditional grids, Prairie, Craftsman or top-row, Diamond, and custom configurations. Depending on the selected window, grid types may include grids between the glass, flat grids, contoured or sculptured grids, and simulated divided lite. Available colors may include white, clay or tan, black, bronze, and additional interior or exterior finishes. Color-matched locks and operating hardware are available, with additional hardware styles and finishes depending on the product. Stephen will explain which combinations are available for your selected window and help match them to your home, priorities, and budget.

Could new windows make drafty, hot, or noisy rooms more comfortable?

The right replacement window and glass package may reduce unwanted air movement, heat transfer, and some outside noise. Results vary based on the home, existing openings, installation conditions, and selected products. Blue Haven does not promise a specific utility-bill reduction or a completely soundproof room.

When will I know the installation dates and expected project length?

Timing depends on product availability, project scope, and conditions at the home. After the order and installation details are confirmed, Stephen will explain the expected schedule and estimated duration before work begins.

Are the product warranty and Blue Haven’s installation-workmanship warranty separate?

Yes. These are two separate forms of coverage. Blue Haven’s Limited Lifetime Workmanship Warranty applies only to qualifying defects caused solely and directly by Blue Haven’s covered installation work. It applies to the original contracting homeowner while that homeowner continuously owns and occupies the original installation residence. It is not transferable unless Blue Haven expressly agrees otherwise in a separate signed writing. Windows, glass, frames, hardware, balances, screens, finishes, and other manufactured products or components are covered under the applicable written manufacturer warranty—not Blue Haven’s workmanship warranty. Manufacturer coverage, eligibility, limitations, labor allowances, exclusions, and transferability vary by product and purchase date. Stephen will provide the applicable written warranty information before you order.

Is financing currently available?

No. Financing is not available at this time. Stephen will review the current payment requirements with you before you decide.

Who performs the installation?

Blue Haven coordinates its installations with independent installation contractors who work directly with Blue Haven on the project and are expected to follow Blue Haven’s installation requirements and quality standards. These installers are independent contractors rather than employees of Blue Haven Windows. Blue Haven remains the homeowner’s point of contact for the contracted project and its applicable workmanship-warranty obligations.

Are cleanup and disposal included, and who handles permits or inspections?

Normal job-site cleanup and removal of the old windows and installation-related debris are included with the installation. Standard replacement-window installations in Tennessee rarely require permits or inspections. If a permit, inspection, or HOA approval is required, the buyer is responsible for obtaining it and paying the associated costs unless the written project agreement says otherwise.

How does the Buy 2 Windows, Get 2 Free promotion work?

Eligible projects are structured in groups of four windows. For each two qualifying windows purchased, the two free windows must be of equal or lesser value. All window styles offered through Blue Haven Windows may qualify, subject to the current written promotion terms included with the homeowner’s proposal.

Who qualifies for Window Cleaning for Life?

Window Cleaning for Life applies only to windows purchased as part of the Advanced Marvin Package and is transferable. Qualifying customers receive one professional exterior window cleaning per year for the covered windows. Unused annual cleanings expire and do not roll over. Screens, tracks, sills, interior glass, and unusually difficult access are excluded. Upper-story access is addressed on a per-project basis. The written proposal or program terms identify the covered windows and any additional scheduling or access requirements.

What happens during the free in-home consultation?

You will discuss the problems you are noticing, review and measure the openings, compare suitable styles and product options, and discuss project scope, pricing, warranty information, promotion eligibility, and anticipated timing. The goal is a clear written estimate and straightforward answers—not a high-pressure appointment.
